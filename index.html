Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Purchase Ledger allows you to enter and manage purchase records, party or customer details, payment information, amounts, notes, and related ledger information.

Ledger information is stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives or stores your ledger records as part of the app's ordinary ledger functionality.

Purchase Ledger may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its related services may process information such as device identifiers, app interactions, diagnostic information, approximate location information, or advertising-related data, subject to Google's settings and policies.

We do not control how third-party advertising services process information. Please review Google's privacy information: Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob information .

4. Internet Access

The app's ledger features are designed to work locally on your device. An internet connection may be required for advertising or other third-party services. The presence of advertising means the app should not be considered entirely offline.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Ledger information remains on your device until you delete it, remove the app, or otherwise clear the app's data. Before uninstalling the app or clearing its data, export a backup if you want to keep your records.

Backup files that you export or share may remain in the locations or services you selected until you delete them there.

6. Children's Privacy

Purchase Ledger is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through a service associated with the app, please contact us so we can review the matter.

We take reasonable steps within the app's design to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and any exported backup files.
